Uzbekistan is one of the largest countries in Central Asia and has a rich historical heritage. Science, culture, and trade have been developing here since ancient times. The capital of Uzbekistan is Tashkent, and its population exceeds 36 million.

The main rivers of Uzbekistan are the Amu Darya and the Syr Darya. The climate is dry and hot, with summer temperatures reaching up to +40°C. Major cities include Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara, and Fergana.

The main sectors of the economy are cotton production, oil and gas extraction, gold and uranium mining, textiles, and agriculture.

Uzbekistan gained independence in 1991. Today, the country is rapidly developing in the areas of education, healthcare, and infrastructure.
